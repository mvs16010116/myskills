## 函数列表
类型| 函数| 用途| 举例  
---|---|---|---  
字符判断  
| [REGEXP](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1476.html>)| 判断字符串和正则表达式是否匹配| REGEXP("Aaaaabbbbc","a*b*c",3)等于trueREGEXP("Aaaaabbbbc","a*b*c",1)等于false  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1455.html>)[ENDWITH](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1455.html>)| 判断字符串结束情况| ENDWITH("FineBI","BI")等于1ENDWITH("FineBI","Fine")等于0ENDWITH("FineBI","bi")等于0  
[STARTWITH](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1477.html>)| 判断字符串 str1 是否以 str2 开始| STARTWITH("FineBI","Fine")等于1 STARTWITH("FineBI","BI")等于0STARTWITH("FineBI","fine")等于0  
[EXACT](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1460.html>)  
| 检测两组文本是否相同| EXACT("Spreadsheet","Spreadsheet")等于1EXACT("Spreadsheet","S preadsheet")等于0EXACT("Spreadsheet","spreadsheet")等于0  
字符串清洗| [](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1454.html>)[FORMAT](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1454.html>)| 格式转换| FORMAT(1234.5,"#,##0.00")=>1234.50FORMAT(1234.5,"#,##0")=>1234  
[TRIM](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1453.html>)| 清除首尾空格| TRIM(" Monthly Report")等于Monthly Report  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1457.html>)[LEFT](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1457.html>)| 截取前几个字符串| LEFT("Finesoftware",8)等于"Finesoft"LEFT("Finesoftware")等于"F"  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1458.html>)[SUBSTITUTE](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1458.html>)| 替换指定字符| SUBSTITUTE("database","base","model")等于“datamodel”SUBSTITUTE("July28,2000","2","1",1)等于“July18,2000”  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1459.html>)[MID](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1459.html>)| 求指定位置字符串| MID("Finemoresoftware",9,8)返回“software”MID("Finemoresoftware",30,5)返回“”（空文本）  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1463.html>)[SPLIT](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1463.html>)| 分割字符串| SPLIT("hello,world,yes",",")=[hello,world,yes]SPLIT("this is very good"," ") = [this,is,very,good]SPLIT("thisisverygood","")=[t,h,i,s,i,s,v,e,r,y,g,o,o,d]  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1464.html>)[UPPER](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1464.html>)| 转为大写字符| UPPER("notes")等于“NOTES”  
[RIGHT](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1465.html>)| 截取后几个字符串| RIGHT("Itisinteresting",6)等于“esting”RIGHT("ShareHolder")等于“r”RIGHT("Hugesale",4)等于“sale”  
[PROPER](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1475.html>)| 替换文本大小写| PROPER("100 percent")等于“100 Percent”PROPER("SpreaDSheEt")等于“Spreadsheet”  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1469.html>)[REPLACE](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1469.html>)| 替换指定位置字符| REPLACE("0123456789",5,4,"*")等于“0123*89”REPLACE("1980",3,2,"99")等于“1999”  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1470.html>)[CONCATENATE](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1470.html>)| 合并字段| CONCATENATE("Average","Price")等于“AveragePrice”CONCATENATE("1","2")等于12。  
[LOWER](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1471.html>)| 大写转小写| LOWER("A.M.10:30")等于“a.m.10:30”LOWER("China")等于“china”  
类型转换  
| [TODOUBLE](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1466.html>)| 将文本转换成 Double 对象| TODOUBLE("123.21")等于123.21  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1461.html>)[TOINTEGER](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1461.html>)| 将文本转换成Integer对象| TOINTEGER("123")等于123  
[NUMTO](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1456.html>)| 求字段的中文表示| NUMTO(2345,true)等于二三四五NUMTO(2345,false)等于二千三百四十五 NUMTO(2345)等于二千三百四十五  
字符信息| [INDEXOF](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1467.html>)| 求指定位置的字符| INDEXOF("FineBI",0)等于'F'INDEXOF("FineBI",2)等于'n'INDEXOF("FineBI",5)等于'I'  
[CHAR](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1473.html>)| 求指定数值的字符| CHAR(88)等于“X”CHAR(45)等于“-”  
[CODE](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1474.html>)| 求数值代码| CODE("S")等于83CODE("Spreadsheet")等于83  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1468.html>)[FIND](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1468.html>)| 求字符串指定字符的位置| FIND("I","Information")等于1FIND("i","Information")等于9FIND("o","Information",2)等于4  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1472.html>)[LEN](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1472.html>)| 求文本串中的字符数长度| LEN("Evermoresoftware")等于16LEN("Evermore software")等于17LEN(["a","b"])等于2  
[](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1462.html>)[REPEAT](<https://help.fanruan.com/finebi7.0/doc-view-1462.html>)| 根据指定的次数重复显示文本| REPEAT("$",4)等于“$$$$”REPEAT("你好",3)等于“你好你好你好”
